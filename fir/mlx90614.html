      The part linearises and compensates on chip, so there is nothing to port: the temperatures
      come straight out of RAM at 0.02 °C per count. It speaks SMBus rather than plain I2C — every
      transaction carries a PEC checksum, which this page verifies, and the bus has to run at
      100 kHz or slower. Dual-zone variants report a second object temperature; a single-zone part
      answers that cell with its error flag set, which is how the page tells them apart.
