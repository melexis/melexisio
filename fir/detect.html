      A scan writes nothing: each address gets a one-byte read, and an address that does not
      acknowledge is reported as empty. Identification then reads a register or two that the
      Melexis libraries themselves use to recognise a part, and the reasoning is shown beside
      every result so a wrong guess is visible rather than silent. Anything that answers is
      listed whether or not it is one of these sensors — an unrecognised device still gets a row,
      with the first bytes it returns. If nothing answers at the selected speed, the sweep runs
      again at 100 kHz, since an SMBus part ignores a faster bus entirely.
